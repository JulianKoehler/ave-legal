# Privacy Policy for the app “Ave – Rosary”

Last updated: 2026/11/15

Ave is built so that as little as possible leaves your device. There is no account and no server of my own. This policy describes which data the app handles, which of it can leave your device, and what rights you have.

## 1. Controller

Julian Köhler Softwareentwicklung & KI-Lösungen
Steller Str. 58
30916 Isernhagen
Germany
E-Mail: julian.koehler.business@gmail.com

No data protection officer has been appointed, as there is no legal obligation to do so.

## 2. Data that stays on your device

Ave stores the following only on your device. I have no access to it, and it is not sent to me or to anyone else:

- your prayers and entries (date, set of mysteries, number of decades, duration),
- your prayer intentions and thanksgivings,
- your first name, if you enter it,
- your settings (language, prayer flow, daily goal, reminders, appearance).

This information can say something about your religious beliefs. That is exactly why it stays on your device. As I never receive it, I do not process it.

If you have switched on a backup in your device settings (for example iCloud Backup or Google backup), the app's data may be part of that backup. The respective provider is responsible for it; you control it in your device settings.

**Deleting:** “Settings › Privacy & data › Delete all data” removes all of the app's data from your device. So does uninstalling the app.

## 3. Microphone

If you switch on “Wait for my voice” when praying in turns, Ave asks for access to the microphone. The app then only measures the volume to tell when you have finished your part. This happens in your device's memory. Nothing is recorded, stored or sent. You can withdraw access at any time in your device settings; the app keeps working, and you tap to continue.

## 4. Reminders

Reminders are local notifications. They are scheduled and triggered on your device. There is no push service, and no device identifier for notifications is sent to a server.

## 5. Usage analytics (only with your consent)

On first launch, Ave asks whether it may count how the app is used. Analytics is off by default and only starts once you expressly agree.

**Purpose:** I would like to understand which features are used, in order to improve the app.

**Which data:** events in the app (for example “prayer started”, “prayer finished”, “setting changed”), linked to a random identifier the app creates itself, plus technical details such as device type, operating system version, app version and language. Not collected: your name, your prayer intentions, thanksgivings and any text you type. Your IP address is not stored.

**Processor:** PostHog Inc., 2261 Market Street #4008, San Francisco, CA 94114, USA. The data is stored in PostHog's EU cloud on servers in Germany (Frankfurt am Main). A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with PostHog. Where access from the USA cannot be ruled out in individual cases, it is covered by the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses.

**Legal basis:** your consent under Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and, because even using a prayer app can say something about your religious beliefs, under Art. 9(2)(a) GDPR, as well as Section 25(1) of the German TDDDG.

**Withdrawal:** you can withdraw your consent at any time under “Settings › Privacy & data” with the “Count usage” switch. From then on no more events are sent. Processing up to that point remains lawful.

**Retention:** events are deleted after 12 months.

## 6. Purchases and subscriptions (Ave Pro)

Praying is free. If you buy Ave Pro, the purchase is handled by your device's store: Apple's App Store or Google Play. I do not receive payment details such as card numbers. Apple and Google are themselves responsible for processing in their stores.

To tell whether Ave Pro is unlocked on your device, and to restore purchases, Ave uses the service RevenueCat.

**Which data:** a random identifier for the app installation created by RevenueCat, details of your purchases in Ave (product, time, status, the store's receipt), the store's country and currency, technical details such as operating system and app version, and your IP address for the duration of the connection. The identifier is created when the app starts, even if you buy nothing. RevenueCat receives neither your name nor your email address, nor any content from the app.

**Processor:** RevenueCat, Inc., 1032 E Brandon Blvd #3003, Brandon, FL 33511, USA. A data processing agreement under Art. 28 GDPR is in place with RevenueCat. The data is processed in the USA; the transfer is covered by the EU Commission's standard contractual clauses.

**Legal basis:** Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR (providing what you bought) and Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interest in reliably determining the purchase status).

**Retention:** as long as needed to provide Ave Pro and to meet statutory retention duties.

## 7. Contact

If you send me an email, I process your address and the content of your message in order to reply (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I delete messages once your request has been dealt with and no statutory retention duties stand in the way.

## 8. What Ave does not do

Ave contains no advertising and no ad networks, builds no profile of you, sells no data and loads no fonts or images from the internet. There is no automated decision-making.

## 9. Children and young people

Anyone can use Ave. You can only give consent to usage analytics yourself from the age of 16 (the age may differ in your country). If you are younger, please leave analytics switched off or ask your parents.

## 10. Your rights

You have the right of access (Art. 15 GDPR), rectification (Art. 16), erasure (Art. 17), restriction of processing (Art. 18), data portability (Art. 20) and the right to object to processing based on legitimate interests (Art. 21). You can withdraw consent at any time (Art. 7(3)).

Please note: because Ave works without an account, I can only find you in the analytics data and at RevenueCat through the random identifier of your app installation. Write to me and I will help you read it out.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority (Art. 77 GDPR), for example the one responsible for me: the State Commissioner for Data Protection of Lower Saxony (Die Landesbeauftragte für den Datenschutz Niedersachsen), Prinzenstraße 5, 30159 Hannover, Germany.

## 11. Changes

If the app or the law changes, I will update this policy. The current version is always available in the app under “Settings › Privacy & data › Privacy policy”.

This is a translation of the German privacy policy. If the two differ, the German version applies.
